ALGORITHM VERIFICATION
==============================================================================

Mathematical Formula:
---------------------
APR = Solve for rate R where:
  Monthly Payment × (1 - (1 + R/12)^(-n)) / (R/12) = Net Loan Amount

Where:
  Net Loan Amount = Loan Amount - Loan Fees - Discount Points
  Monthly Payment = Calculated on full loan amount at note rate
  n = Loan term in months

Method:
-------
✅ Newton's Method iteration
✅ Convergence tolerance: 0.0001%
✅ Maximum iterations: 100
✅ Bounds checking: 0-100%
✅ NaN/infinite guards present
✅ Best approximation fallback

Example Calculation:
--------------------
Input:
  - Loan Amount: $450,000
  - Interest Rate: 5.25%
  - Term: 30 years
  - Loan Fees: $4,500
  - Discount Points: 0.5%
