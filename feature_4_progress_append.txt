ALGORITHM VERIFICATION:
=======================

Method: Logarithmic solution for loan term
Formula: n = -log(1 - (P×r/M)) / log(1+r)

Where:
- P = Loan Amount
- M = Monthly Payment
- r = Monthly Interest Rate (annual/100/12)
- n = Total months

Example:
- Loan: $200,000
- Payment: $1,500
- Rate: 6.5%
- Result: ~19.78 years
