MATHEMATICAL VERIFICATION:
==========================

Formula: M = P * [r(1+r)^n] / [(1+r)^n - 1]

Where:
- P = Principal ($300,000)
- r = Monthly interest rate (6.5% / 12 = 0.005417)
- n = Number of payments (30 years × 12 = 360)

Calculation:
M = 300000 * [0.005417(1.005417)^360] / [(1.005417)^360 - 1]
M = 300000 * [0.005417 × 7.0125] / [7.0125 - 1]
M = 300000 * 0.03799 / 6.0125
M = 300000 * 0.0063207
M = $1,896.20
